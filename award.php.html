Best Paper Award
The Technical Committee 6 (TC6) of the International Fedaration For Information Processing (IFIP) is pleased to offer two Middleware'08 Best Paper Awards.

  1. The first Best Paper Award goes to:

    Ningfang Mi1, Giuliano Casale1, Ludmila Cherkasova2 and Evgenia Smirni1

    1 College of William and Mary, Williamsburg, VA 23187, USA
    2 Hewlett-Packard Laboratories, Palo Alto, CA 94304, USA

    for the paper

    Burstiness in Multi-Tier Applications:
    Symptoms, Causes, and New Models



  2. And the second Best Paper Award goes to:

    Gokul Soundararajan and Cristiana Amza

    Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Toronto

    for the paper

    Towards End-to-End Quality of Service:
    Controlling I/O Interference in Shared Storage Server
