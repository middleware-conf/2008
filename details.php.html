On Monday and Tuesday, the registration desk can be found in Salons Georges where the workshops will take place. On Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, the registration desk will be located in College de Valk where the main conference will take place.

Thursday:8:00 - 18:00(College De Valk)
Friday:8:00 - 17:00(College De Valk)
